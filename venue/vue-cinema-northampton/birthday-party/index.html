How a cinema birthday party works

Choose a date and time

Weekend mornings and school holidays are the busiest times for parties, so enquire a few weeks ahead. Weekday after-school slots are often easier to secure.

Pick the film

Choose from the films showing that week. For younger guests, check the certificate and running time, as most parties last the length of the film plus 30 minutes.

Arrive early

Aim to arrive 20 to 30 minutes before the film so everyone can collect their popcorn and drinks and get seated together.

Birthday parties at Vue Northampton: FAQs

How much is a birthday party at Vue Northampton?

Birthday party packages at Vue Northampton start from £12 per guest, including a film from the current listings, popcorn and a drink. Kids' party packages start from £10 per child. Private hire of a whole screen is priced on request.

What is included in a Vue birthday party?

A seat for every guest in a reserved screen, a film from the current listings, regular popcorn and a soft drink each, and a free ticket for the birthday guest.

Can we have a screen to ourselves?

Yes. Screens 6, 7 and 8 seat 50 to 74 guests and are the most popular for private birthday parties. Mornings before public showings are the easiest to secure.

How many guests can come?

Party packages start from 10 guests. The smallest private screen seats 50, and the largest seats 310.

Can we bring a cake?

Ask Vue Northampton when you enquire. Many cinemas let you bring a birthday cake to enjoy in the foyer before or after the film.
