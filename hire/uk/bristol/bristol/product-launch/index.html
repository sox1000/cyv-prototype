Product launch venue FAQs

What makes a good product launch venue in Bristol?

Pick a space that suits the product: a cinema screen for anything with a film or demo, a historic venue for a premium brand, or a stadium suite when you need room to exhibit. Check AV, power and load-in access before you secure the date.

Can we launch a product at a cinema?

Yes. Showcase Cinema de Lux Bristol Avonmeads hires out screens for presentations and reveals, with the screen, sound and seating already in place.

Are there unusual launch venues near Bristol?

Yes. SS Great Britain and We The Curious are in the city, and Longleat, 24 miles away, offers its house and grounds for launches, filming and team events.
