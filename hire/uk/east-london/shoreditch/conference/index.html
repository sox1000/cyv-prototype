Conference Venues in Shoreditch

Updated November 2026 · 22 venues listed

Shoreditch suits events where people arrive by train: three stations and four Underground and Overground lines sit within a ten-minute walk, and Liverpool Street connects directly to Heathrow, Stansted and the City. Venues run from a restored Victorian town hall to warehouse spaces and hotel meeting rooms.

Choosing between Shoreditch, the City and King's Cross

Shoreditch is cheaper per delegate than the City a few streets south, with more characterful spaces. For delegates coming from the north, King's Cross is one stop from Old Street on the Northern line; for those coming from the east of England, Liverpool Street is the terminus.

Conference venue FAQs

Which stations are closest to Shoreditch conference venues?

Most venues are within a 10-minute walk of Shoreditch High Street (London Overground), Old Street (Northern line) or Liverpool Street (Elizabeth line, Central line and mainline trains). Use "Near" in the filters to sort venues by distance from any of them.

How do delegates get to Shoreditch from the airports?

London City Airport is 5.8 miles away. From Heathrow, the Elizabeth line runs direct to Liverpool Street in about 40 minutes, and the Stansted Express reaches Liverpool Street in about 50 minutes.

What is the largest conference venue in Shoreditch?

Shoreditch Town Hall's assembly hall seats up to 800 theatre-style, and Village Underground holds up to 700 standing.
