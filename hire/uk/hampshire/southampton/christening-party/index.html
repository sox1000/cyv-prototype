Planning a christening party in Southampton

Stay close to the church

The biggest favour you can do your guests is a short journey from font to food. Many families at St Mary's, Highfield Church or St Joseph's choose a venue within a mile or two of the ceremony — the radius filter narrows the list to your parish.

Think afternoon, not evening

Most receptions run late morning to mid-afternoon, which works in your favour on price: many Southampton venues charge less for daytime hire, and some waive it against a food and drink minimum. Afternoon tea and finger buffets are the popular choices.

Match the room to your guest list

Thirty to eighty guests is typical. Leave headroom above confirmed numbers — you'll want space for buffet tables, a spot to park prams, and floor for small children to roam without ending up under the cake table.

Christening venue FAQs

How much does it cost to hire a christening party venue in Southampton?

Room hire typically starts from around £25 for community halls and pub function rooms, rising to £300–£900 for hotel suites. Many venues waive the hire fee entirely against a minimum food and drink spend.

What size venue do I need for a christening reception?

Most receptions fall between 30 and 80 guests. Aim for a capacity a little above your guest list so there's space for buffet tables, a pram park and room for children.

Can I find a venue close to my church?

Yes — the search radius filter narrows results to within one or five miles of your postcode, keeping the journey short for guests with babies and elderly relatives.
