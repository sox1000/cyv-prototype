Planning a conference in Southampton

Start with how delegates will travel

Southampton Central has direct trains from London Waterloo, Bristol and Birmingham, and Southampton Airport Parkway sits next to the terminal. Venues near either station suit national events; out-of-town hotels with free parking suit regional teams who drive.

Plan for the breakouts, not just the plenary

Most conferences need one room for everyone and two or three smaller rooms for workshops. Check the venue's other rooms as well as its largest, and ask whether lunch is served in a separate space so the plenary room doesn't have to be turned around.

Compare day delegate rates like for like

A day delegate rate usually covers room hire, refreshments, lunch and standard AV. Ask each venue exactly what is included, and whether the rate changes for smaller numbers or residential stays.

Conference venue FAQs

How much does a conference venue in Southampton cost?

Day delegate rates at the 31 venues listed start from £35 per person and typically run to £65 at city-centre hotels. Rates usually include room hire, refreshments, lunch and standard AV.

What is the largest conference venue in Southampton?

Utilita Bowl seats up to 1,000 delegates theatre-style, and Southampton Guildhall holds up to 1,500 for larger events.

Which Southampton conference venues have bedrooms?

Harbour Hotel Southampton, New Place Hotel and Utilita Bowl all have bedrooms on site. Use the "Bedrooms on site" filter to see the full list.
