About ChooseYourVenue

Helping people find the right venue since 2004

ChooseYourVenue is an independent UK venue directory. We list hotels, function rooms, halls, restaurants and unique spaces for every kind of occasion, from christening parties and wakes to conferences and Christmas parties.

Search is free, and so is every enquiry. You contact the venue directly, with no booking fee and no middleman taking a cut of your event.

What we do

One place to compare venues by location, occasion, capacity and price, in plain English.

1

For people planning an event

Browse venues by town and occasion, compare rooms and capacities side by side, and send free enquiries straight to the venues you like.

2

For venues

A listing puts your rooms, packages and offers in front of people actively planning an event in your area. Enquiries come to you directly.

3

For greener events

ECOsmart marks venues that have shown how they cut waste and energy, so you can choose a venue that shares your values.

How it works

  1. Search your area

    Pick a town and an occasion to see venues near you, with capacities, room hire and what's included.

  2. Shortlist and compare

    Save the venues you like and compare them side by side, from room sizes to parking and catering.

  3. Enquire for free

    Send one enquiry to the venues on your shortlist. They reply to you directly with availability and prices.
