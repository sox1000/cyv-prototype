Hilton Bournemouth

Venue description

Welcome to the 4-star Hilton Bournemouth, perfectly designed for a memorable conference or event. The hotel offers 177 stylish rooms with panoramic windows over the heart of the town, and 12 event spaces for hire — including a 306-guest ballroom with a large pre-function area. Four meeting rooms can be combined into one with a connecting balcony, all designed with a unique, playful character.

Dining centres on the hotel's signature restaurant, Schpoons & Forx, and on-site parking includes provision for electric vehicles — recognising the growing demand for sustainable travel options.
