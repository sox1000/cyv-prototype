How many guests can the Phoxtrott Ballroom hold?

The Phoxtrott Ballroom holds up to 360 guests theatre-style. For a seated banquet it takes 270, and for a standing reception 204.

What layouts can the Phoxtrott Ballroom be set up in?

Theatre for 360, banquet for 270, dinner for 240, cabaret for 208, reception for 204 and classroom for 146.

How much does it cost to hire the Phoxtrott Ballroom?

Room hire starts from £600. The final price depends on your date, timings and catering, so ask Hilton Bournemouth for an exact quote. There is no booking fee.

How big is the Phoxtrott Ballroom?

The room measures 341 square metres (about 3,670 sq ft).

What events is the Phoxtrott Ballroom suitable for?

Hilton Bournemouth offers the Phoxtrott Ballroom for birthday parties, Christmas parties, wedding receptions, conferences, gala dinners, award ceremonies and charity balls.

What other rooms are there at Hilton Bournemouth?

Hilton Bournemouth has 11 other rooms for hire, including Rule, Centrefold, Balance & Blot (up to 112 guests), Centrefold, Balance & Blot (up to 84) and Balance & Blot (up to 56).
