Vue Northampton

Sixfields Leisure, Upton Way, Northampton NN5 5QA

Private screen hire at Vue Northampton

Vue Northampton at Sixfields has eight screens, from a 50-seat screen that is ideal for a child's birthday party to a 310-seat auditorium for company screenings and conferences. Choose a film from the current listings, add popcorn and drinks, and the screen is yours.

Free parking at Sixfields and quick access from junction 16 of the M1 make it easy for guests coming from across the county.

Vue Northampton FAQs

How much does a birthday party at Vue Northampton cost?

Party packages at Vue Northampton start from £12 per guest, including a film from the current listings, popcorn and a drink. Private hire of a whole screen is priced on request.

Can we have a screen to ourselves?

Yes. Screens 6, 7 and 8 seat between 50 and 74 guests and are the most popular for private birthday and kids' parties.

How many seats are in the largest screen?

Screen 1 seats 310, which suits conferences, awards and company screenings.

What events can we hold at Vue Northampton?

Vue Northampton hosts birthday parties, kids' parties, private screenings, conferences and Christmas parties.

What other cinemas are near Vue Northampton?

Showcase Cinema de Lux Leicester is 33 miles away and also offers private screen hire.
