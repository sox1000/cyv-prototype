Showcase Cinemas FAQs

Can you hire a Showcase cinema screen privately?

Yes. Showcase cinemas offer private screen hire for conferences, awards, product launches, private screenings and parties. Mornings before public showings are the easiest to secure.

Which Showcase cinemas have event packages?

All the Showcase cinemas listed here take private hire enquiries through ChooseYourVenue. Each venue page shows its screens, seats and packages.
