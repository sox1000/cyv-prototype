Planning a christening reception

Book near the church

Keep the journey from font to food short — use the town pages above and their radius filters to stay within a mile or two of the ceremony. It matters most for guests travelling with babies and older relatives.

Think lunchtime, not evening

Most receptions run late morning to mid-afternoon, and many venues charge less for daytime hire — or waive the room fee against a food and drink minimum. Afternoon tea and finger buffets are the popular choices, and highchairs are almost always available on request.

Size the room honestly

Thirty to eighty guests is typical. Choose a capacity a little above your list so there's room for buffet tables, a pram park and children on the move.

Christening venue FAQs

How much does a christening venue cost?

Room hire ranges from around £25 for halls and pub function rooms to several hundred pounds for hotel suites — and many venues waive hire fees against a minimum spend for daytime events.

How far ahead should we book?

Six to eight weeks suits most venues, but Saturday and Sunday lunchtimes book out earlier — enquire with two or three venues as soon as your church date is confirmed.
