How private cinema hire works

Pick your time

Mornings and early afternoons, before public showings start, are the easiest to secure and usually the best value. Evening and weekend private hire is possible but books up quickly, especially in December.

Choose the screen size

Small screens of 30 to 60 seats suit children's parties and team screenings. Large screens of 200 seats or more work for conferences and awards, often with a stage at the front.

Add food and drinks

Party packages usually include popcorn, a drink and sometimes a meal. Corporate packages can add tea and coffee on arrival, lunch and use of the bar.

Private cinema hire FAQs

How much does it cost to hire a cinema screen?

Private screen hire at the cinemas listed starts from about £12 per guest for a children's party package, and from around £400 for a small screen for a corporate morning. Prices depend on the screen size, the day and the time, so enquire for an exact quote.

Can you hire a cinema for a birthday party?

Yes. Many cinemas, including Vue and Showcase, offer birthday and kids' party packages with a private or reserved screen, a film of your choice from the current listings, and food or popcorn and drinks.

Can we use a cinema screen for a conference or presentation?

Yes. Most screens can connect a laptop for slides and video, and some have a stage and microphones. Mornings before the first public showing are the easiest to secure.

Can we show our own film?

Often, yes, for private and corporate hire. Ask the cinema about formats and licensing when you enquire.
