Christmas Party Nights 2026

at Hilton Bournemouth · Terrace Road, Bournemouth, Dorset

From £49 per person

About the offer

Join other parties in the Phoxtrott Ballroom for a full night out: a glass of fizz on arrival, a three-course festive dinner, then the DJ takes over until 1am. It suits teams and groups of 8 to 60 who want a proper party without hiring the whole room.

Larger groups can take a private room instead. The hotel's events team will suggest the right space when you enquire, from the 40-seat Poise boardroom to the full ballroom for 360.

What's included

  • Glass of prosecco or soft drink on arrival
  • Three-course festive dinner with tea, coffee and mince pies
  • Crackers, novelties and table decorations
  • DJ and disco until 1am, with late bar
  • Reduced overnight rate for party guests, subject to availability
