Special offer FAQs

How do I claim an offer?

Choose Enquire on the offer. Your enquiry goes straight to the venue and names the offer, so they can confirm availability and the price you saw. There is no booking fee.

What's the difference between "ends" and the event dates?

The event dates are when your event must take place to get the offer. The end date is the last day to confirm your booking. Some offers need confirming weeks before the event itself.

Are these offers still available?

Yes. Only live offers are shown, and each one is removed the day it ends. Availability on your date is up to the venue, so enquire early for popular December weekends.
