Listing FAQs

How do enquiries reach me?

Each enquiry goes straight to the email address on your listing, with the guest's details, date and numbers. There's no booking fee and we take no commission.

Can I list more than one venue?

Yes. Each venue has its own listing, and groups with several venues can manage them from one account. Ask us about a group rate.

Can I add special offers?

Yes. Offers appear on your venue page and on the special offers page, and drop off automatically the day they end.

What is ECOsmart?

ECOsmart is our accreditation for venues that can show how they reduce waste and energy at events. Accredited venues carry the ECOsmart badge in search results.
